SECOND SOURCE / SRC001 / 2027

DRIP

MARTIN SOLLI

Drip is a ten-track album by Norwegian producer Martin Solli, developed over five years between Tromsø and Oslo. The record brings together material written and produced at different points during this period, collected into a single full-length release.

Although Solli has previously released music under different aliases and worked as a producer for other artists, Drip is his first solo album under his own name.

Rather than documenting one particular recording period, the album brings together material from different stages of his work. The ten tracks are presented as a complete sequence, beginning with the title track and ending with Dream Scenario.

The album was written, produced and mixed by Solli, with additional synthesizer and bass contributions from Peter Estdahl on Sage Counsel and Dream Scenario. Mastering was handled by Pelle Loopik at Time Item Studio.

Drip is the first release on Second Source, an independent outlet for audio and visual experimentation based in Oslo.

Available on vinyl and digitally in 2027.
