SECOND SOURCE / SRC001

DRIP

MARTIN SOLLI

SPRING 2027 / Vinyl & Digital

Drip is a ten-track album by Norwegian producer Martin Solli, developed over five years between Tromsø and Oslo. It is his first solo album under his own name.

Solli has previously released music under different aliases and worked as a producer for other artists across electronic music, hip-hop and pop.

Drip is the first release on Second Source and will be available on vinyl and digitally in spring 2027.
