SRC001

DRIP

MARTIN SOLLI

Drip is a 10-track electronic LP by Martin Solli, developed over five years between Tromsø and Oslo.

The record brings together material written and produced across different periods, moving between house, downtempo and electronica. It is the first release on Second Source.
