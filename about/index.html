ABOUT / CONTACT

Second Source is an independent outlet for audio and visual experimentation, based in Oslo, Norway.

The label provides a space for releasing records and exploring different forms of visual work, without being limited to a particular genre or medium.

The first release is Drip (SRC001), a ten-track album by Martin Solli.
